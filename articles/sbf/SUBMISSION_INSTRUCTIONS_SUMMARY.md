# REVISTA BRASILEIRA DE ENSINO DE FÍSICA (RBEF) - SUBMISSION INSTRUCTIONS SUMMARY

**Source:** `/articles/sbf/instructions/instructions.html`
**Journal:** Revista Brasileira de Ensino de Física (RBEF) — Published by Sociedade Brasileira de Física (SBF)
**Platform:** ScholarOne

---

## SUBMISSION REQUIREMENTS & SCOPE

### Scope & Article Types
- Manuscripts must be **original, unpublished** works representing relevant contributions to physics education
- Articles must **not** be under evaluation at other journals or previously published
- Articles in **Portuguese, Spanish, and English** are accepted
- All manuscripts undergo editorial review and peer evaluation

### Acceptable Article Categories
- Theoretical and experimental aspects of Physics
- Instructional materials and methods
- Curriculum development
- Physics teaching research
- History and philosophy of Physics
- Teacher education and professional development
- Educational policy
- Pedagogical innovations
- Interdisciplinary approaches relating to physics teaching

### NOT Accepted
- State-of-the-art literature reviews (unless systematic reviews with rigorous, explicit, reproducible methodology, comprehensive databases, critical analysis, and connection to physics teaching)
- Generic educational discussions unconnected to physics

## FILE FORMATS & TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

### Acceptable File Formats
- `.tex` (LaTeX) ✓
- `.doc` (Word) ✓
- `.pdf` ✗ (NOT permitted — submission system generates PDF automatically)

### LaTeX (.tex) Requirements
- **Encoding:** UTF-8 with accents
- **Preamble:** All class declarations, styles, and other commands must be in preamble
- **TikZ:** NOT permitted
- **Text Quality:** Must be clean, no commented-out passages
- **References:** Must use `\bibitem` format
- **Template:** No official template provided

### Word (.doc) Requirements
- **Equations:** Must use Word's equation editor tool (not manual formatting)
- No custom formatting that cannot be converted

## MANUSCRIPT CONTENT STRUCTURE

### Languages & Bilingual Requirement
- **Bilingual requirement:** Titles, abstracts, and keywords MUST appear in **BOTH** Portuguese AND English (OR Spanish AND English, OR Spanish AND Portuguese)
- Authors indicate primary submission language
- Both language versions must be provided simultaneously in article text

### Word Count & Character Limits
- **Recommended:** 5,000 to 15,000 words
- **Equivalent:** Approximately 30,000 to 105,000 characters with spaces
- **Flexibility:** Possible depending on manuscript nature (Editor-in-Chief discretion)

### Title (Título)
- Must be provided in both article language and English/Portuguese
- Clear and concise

### Abstract (Resumo)
- Must be provided in both article language and English/Portuguese
- Clarity and accuracy evaluated by reviewers

### Keywords (Palavras-chave)
- **3 to 6 keywords** required
- Must be provided in both article language and English/Portuguese

### Equations
- Must be in the **article body** (not separate files)
- Reference as "equação (1)" or "equation (1)"

  - Other functions in taxonomy

## REFERENCES & CITATIONS

### Reference Format Requirements
- Complete references at **END of article**
- Listed in **order of appearance** in text
- **Author names:** Clear (use "et al." only after 10th author)
- **Journal name:** Full name required
- **Volume number:** Bold
- **Page numbers:** Included
- **Publication year:** In parentheses

### Reference Format Examples by Type

**Journal Articles:**
```
J.A. Silva, Phys. Rev. B 26, 15 (1982).
```

**Books:**
```
J.A. Silva, Nome do Livro (Nome da Editora, Cidade, Ano), v. 2.
```

**Book Chapters:**
```
J.A. Silva, em: Nome do Livro, editado por C. Brown (Nome da Editora, Cidade, Ano).
```

**Conference Proceedings:**
```
J.A. Silva, em: Anais do XXXIV Simpósio Nacional de Ensino de Física (Cidade, Ano).
```

**Theses/Dissertations:**
```
J.A. Silva, Nome da Tese. Tese de Doutorado, Universidade, Cidade (Ano).
```

**Preprints:**
```
P.J. Miranda, M.S. Baptista e S.E.S. Pinto, arXiv:1306.2537 (2013).
```

Authors must declare:

## ETHICS & RESEARCH INTEGRITY

### Misconduct Definitions
- **Plagiarism:** Appropriation of ideas, processes, results, or words without proper credit (including self-plagiarism)
- **Falsification:** Manipulation of materials, equipment, processes; omission of data/results misrepresenting research
- **Fabrication:** Invention of data or results

### Plagiarism Detection
- All RBEF submissions verified by anti-plagiarism software

### Policies
- RBEF aligned with **Committee on Publication Ethics (COPE)** principles
- **Manuscripts cannot** be submitted to other journals while under RBEF evaluation
- Detected inadequate research practices or misconduct = **automatic rejection**
- Post-publication misconduct detected = **retraction required** after investigation
- **Simultaneous submission** to multiple journals = automatic rejection if detected

### Institutional Ethics Approval
- **Generally NOT required** by RBEF
- **Exception:** Research involving human subjects requiring ethical evaluation:
  - Questionnaires
  - Interviews
  - Classroom observation
  - Analysis of identifiable data
- In these cases: RBEF may request ethics committee approval or equivalent declaration
- Authors encouraged to include ethical disclaimers when applicable

### Gender & Sex Equity
- Editorial team and authors must observe **SAGER** (Sex and Gender Equity in Research) guidelines
- SAGER guidelines for:
  - Study design reporting
  - Data analysis
  - Results and interpretation
- RBEF observes gender equity in editorial board and reviewer composition

## PEER REVIEW PROCESS

### Editorial Screening
- Manuscripts undergo initial screening by Editor-in-Chief
- Results in:
  - **Acceptance** for peer review (ad hoc review), OR
  - **Rejection in limine** if:
    - Not properly formatted
    - Outside journal scope
    - Below quality criteria

### Peer Review Model
- **Non-blind review** (NOT double-blind)
- Reviewers have access to authorship
- Authors do NOT have access to reviewer identities
- Reviewers required to declare no conflicts of interest

### Reviewer Selection
Ad hoc reviewers must have:
- Doctoral degree
- Recognized academic experience
- Recognized scientific production in manuscript topic/area

- Authors may suggest reviewers with expertise
- Number of reviewers determined by availability, complexity, topic specificity

### Reviewer Evaluation Criteria
Reviewers assess:
- Level of interest
- Originality
- General quality (compared to other RBEF publications)
- Abstract clarity and accuracy
- Problem conciseness and relevance
- Methodological description
- Results and interpretation relationship
- Conclusion adequacy
- Reference appropriateness
- Text length appropriateness
- Number of tables, figures, and diagrams

### Reviewer Recommendations
- Accept without corrections
- Accept with minor revisions required
- Accept with major revisions required
- Reject and suggest resubmission
- Reject definitively

### Final Decision
- **Editor-in-Chief has final authority**
- Decision based on:
  - Reviewer evaluations
  - Journal scope preservation
  - Publication quality preservation

### Post-Acceptance
- Proofs sent to authors for verification before publication
- Reviewers may offer direct dialogue with corresponding author (optional)
- Approved reviews may be published with or without reviewer identification (optional)
